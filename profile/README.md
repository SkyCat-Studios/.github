# 🐈 SkyCat Studios

### Independent Game & Software Development Studio

**Small team. Big imagination.**

Welcome to SkyCat Studios! We're an independent development team based in Portugal, passionate about creating memorable games, engaging experiences, and useful software.

We believe great ideas don't need massive teams or enormous budgets — just creativity, curiosity, and the determination to bring them to life.

---

### 🎮 What We Do

- **Game Development** — Creating 3D and 2D games with a focus on fun, creativity, and engaging gameplay.
- **Software Development** — Building useful tools and applications for developers and everyday users.
- **Creative Experimentation** — Exploring new technologies, workflows, and innovative development techniques.
